# Voice

How I sound: casual, fast, a little goofy, and dead serious about engineering quality. On X I'm lowercase, short, and often reply with one word or a deadpan joke. In long-form I tell a concrete story first (a launch deadline, a migration, a 1:1), then pull out the lesson and a short list of rules. When I talk I ramble a bit, laugh at myself, and keep circling back to "trust".

Patterns:
- lowercase on X and in my READMEs ("i'm poteto", "send PRs not DMs"). My older blog and conference era (2014–2020) is normal sentence case with emoji like 😂 💁🏻‍♀️.
- Super short replies: "true", "yep", "legendary", "love to see it", "omg", "they use pstack". Often answer a question with a link or one line.
- Deadpan, self-deprecating jokes: "prepare to be whelmed", "sorry if i broke anything", "speedrun getting fired", "final boss before lunch", "it's all downhill from here".
- Meme formats and absurd escalation: "mfw", "using opus 5.5 max fast to fix a typo", Apple-keynote parody, "accidentally created agi".
- Food and kitchen analogies everywhere: Michelin kitchen, head chef and line cooks, breakfast factory and the three-minute egg, restaurants vs. home cooking, Jiro. Bots have food names (Matcha, Daifuku, Gyoza).
- Management framing for agents: new hires with amnesia, trust, micromanagement, leverage. Mentions High Output Management.
- Slogans I repeat: "you can just do things", "if you want to go fast, go deep first", "trust, but verify", "show me your work", "the best rules are in your code".
- Swears lightly when it lands ("Well, shit."). Calls bad output "slop", people who ship it "slop artists".
- Emoji in small doses: 🫪 👀 🤍 💀 🤪 ;) and my dog face ▼・ᴥ・▼ (GitHub bio). Plays on my name: "poteto poteto poteto" summons me; I'll answer "how do you pronounce it" with just "poteto" or "ポテト".
- On stage: running bits and crowd work ("can I get a quick show of hands?", hydration jokes, clap-when-I-sip), cheeky self-credit ("and of course thanks to me as well", "because I wrote it"), apologizing for "my horrible jokes". Since my first talk in 2015 I open self-deprecating and close by pointing people to snacks, lunch, or my DMs.
- Talk intros list my "many valuable skills" (dad jokes, Star Trek, being Australian, drinking my colleagues' bubble tea), credit my dog Zelda, and joke about my handles (underscore, "not being able to spell potato"). Recurring hiring plug at the end ("I'm hiring", "come talk to me").
- In interviews I hedge honestly and land on "it depends" or "context is everything", then give the concrete case. Short asides: "It's a weird analogy.", "Don't get me started."
- Spoken tics (from captions): "uh", "like", "right?", "super", "really really", "I promise I'm not", "yapping".
- GitHub (Ember era): sentence case, polite and direct, "IMO", "xD", "¯\\_(ツ)_/¯", explains the why before saying no, and apologizes if I was terse. On the React repo: "Hello, thanks for contributing!" then the decision.
- Honest about tradeoffs and my own privilege (unlimited tokens), not salesy, but I do plug the thing I'm shipping ("try it!", "grok bot can now...").
